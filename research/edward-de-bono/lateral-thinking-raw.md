## Quotes & evidence

### Q1 — Lateral is generative, vertical is selective (the core split)
> "Lateral thinking is not a substitute for vertical thinking. Both are required They are complementary. Lateral thinking is generative. Vertical thinking is selective."
>
> "Vertical thinking selects a pathway by excluding other pathways. Lateral [thinking] does not select but seeks to open up other pathways. ... With lateral thinking one goes on generating as many approaches as one can even after one has found a promising one. With vertical thinking one is trying to select the best approach but with lateral thinking one is generating different approaches for the sake of generating them."
>
> — *Lateral Thinking: A Textbook of Creativity* (1970), ch. "Difference between lateral and vertical thinking" (PDF 추출 lines 281–283, 1024–1035)

**Context**: 두 사고 양식의 차이를 열거하는 비교 챕터의 첫 항목. 발산(lateral)이 판단·선택(vertical)과 별개의 동작임을 못 박는 대목.

### Q2 — Movement, not direction: "moves in order to generate a direction"
> "Vertical thinking moves only if there is a direction in which to move, lateral thinking moves in order to generate a direction."
>
> "With lateral thinking one moves for the sake of moving. One does not have to be moving towards something, one may be moving away from something. It is the movement or change that matters. ... The vertical thinker says: 'I know what I am looking for.' The lateral thinker says: 'I am looking but I won't know what I am looking for until I have found it.'"
>
> — *Lateral Thinking: A Textbook of Creativity* (1970), ch. "Difference between lateral and vertical thinking" (lines 1040–1063)

**Context**: 비교 챕터 두 번째 항목. movement 개념의 원형 — 목표를 향한 이동이 아니라 패턴을 흔들기 위한 이동.

### Q3 — Need not be right at each step; "build a bridge" / "no negative"
> "The very essence of vertical thinking is that one must be right at each step. ... In lateral thinking however one does not have to be right at each step provided the conclusion is right. It is like building a bridge. The parts do not have to be self-supporting at every stage but when the last part is fitted into place the bridge suddenly becomes self-supporting."
>
> "With lateral thinking one realizes that a pattern cannot be restructured from within itself but only as the result of some outside influence. So one welcomes outside influences for their provocative action. The more irrelevant such influences are the more chance there is of altering the established pattern."
>
> — *Lateral Thinking: A Textbook of Creativity* (1970), ch. "Difference..." (lines 1107–1148)

**Context**: 같은 비교 챕터. "단계마다 옳아야 한다"는 vertical의 제약을 lateral이 어떻게 푸는지, 그리고 무관한 외부 자극을 환영하는 이유(패턴은 안에서 못 바꾼다).

### Q4 — Lateral thinking is the description of a *process*, not a result (de-mystifying creativity)
> "Lateral thinking is closely related to creativity. But whereas creativity is too often only the description of a result, lateral thinking is the description of a process. One can only admire a result but one can learn to use a process. There is about creativity a mystique of talent and intangibles. ... In order to be able to use creativity one must rid it of this aura of mystique and regard it as a way of using the mind — a way of handling information. This is what lateral thinking is about."
>
> — *Lateral Thinking: A Textbook of Creativity* (1970), Introduction (lines 247–260)

**Context**: 서문. 드 보노가 창의를 '재능'에서 '절차'로 옮기겠다고 선언하는 핵심 동기 진술.

### Q5 — "You cannot dig a hole in a different place by digging the same hole deeper"
> "You cannot dig a hole in a different place by digging the same hole deeper. Vertical thinking is used to dig the same hole deeper. Lateral thinking is used to dig a hole in a different place."
>
> — *Lateral Thinking: A Textbook of Creativity* (1970), Introduction (lines 298–300)

**Context**: 서문. lateral과 vertical의 상보성을 압축한 가장 유명한 비유.

### Q6 — The mind as a self-organizing, self-maximizing patterning system
> "The mind is not a machine, however, but a special environment which allows information to organize itself into patterns. This self-organizing, self-maximizing, memory system is very good at creating patterns and that is the effectiveness of mind."
>
> "But inseparable from the great usefulness of a patterning system are certain limitations. In such a system it is easy to combine patterns or to add to them but it is extremely difficult to restructure them for the patterns control attention. ... Lateral thinking involves restructuring, escape and the provocation of new patterns."
>
> — *Lateral Thinking: A Textbook of Creativity* (1970), Introduction (lines 231–246)

**Context**: 서문. lateral thinking의 정당화 근거 — 마음이 패턴 형성 시스템이라 "안에서는" 재구조화가 안 되고, 그래서 도발이 필요하다는 메커니즘.

### Q7 — Why humor and insight come "from the side" (asymmetry of patterns)
> "As with the plastic pieces there is often an alternative way of arranging available information. This means that there can be a switch over to another arrangement. Usually this switch over is sudden. If the switch over is temporary it gives rise to humour. If the switch over is permanent it gives rise to insight. It is interesting that the reaction to an insight solution is often laughter even when there is nothing funny about the solution itself."
>
> "In each of these situations an expectation is generated by the way the information is put together. Then suddenly this expectation is thwarted but at once one sees that the unexpected development is another way of putting things together."
>
> — *Lateral Thinking: A Textbook of Creativity* (1970), ch. "The way the mind works" → "Humour and insight" (lines 912–933)

**Context**: 마음 작동 모델 챕터. 유머=일시적 패턴 전환, 통찰=영구적 패턴 전환이라는 동일 메커니즘. 측면(다른 진입점)에서 들어와야 전환이 일어난다는 근거.

### Q8 — Asymmetry of patterns: "very difficult to think forward asymmetrically"
> "It is interesting that answer F is so rare for in hindsight it seems the easiest of them all (the explanation is that it is very difficult to think forward asymmetrically ...)."
>
> "To start at the wrong end and work backwards is quite a well-known problem solving technique. The reason why it is effective is that the line of thought may be quite different from what it would have been had one started at the beginning. ... It is convenient to do so since the solution is often clearly defined. But one can start at any point. If there is no obvious point then one must be generated."
>
> — *Lateral Thinking: A Textbook of Creativity* (1970), ch. "Choice of entry point and attention area" (lines 4541–4562)

**Context**: 진입점(entry point) 챕터. 패턴은 비대칭이라 정방향이 막히고, 일부러 다른 끝/다른 진입점을 잡아야 한다는 절차적 근거.

### Q9 — PO is to lateral thinking what NO is to logical thinking
> "PO is to lateral thinking what NO is to logical thinking. NO is a rejection tool. PO is an insight restructuring tool. ... Logic could be said to be the management of NO. ... Lateral thinking could be said to be the management of PO just as logical thinking is the management of NO."
>
> "NO is a judgement device. PO is an anti-judgement device. NO works within the framework of reason. PO works outside that framework. ... Lateral thinking is not irrational but arational. Lateral thinking deals with the patterning of information not with the judgement of those patterns. Lateral thinking is prereason. PO is never a judgement device. PO is a construction device. PO is a patterning device."
>
> — *Lateral Thinking: A Textbook of Creativity* (1970), ch. "The new word po" (lines 5715–5738)

**Context**: PO 챕터. PO라는 단어를 발명한 이유와 그 역할 — 판단(NO)의 정반대 자리에서 정보를 재배열하는 도구.

### Q10 — The three functions of PO; PO delays judgement (the "middle course")
> "An arrangement of information is usually judged as soon as it comes about. The judgement results in one of two verdicts: 'This is permissible', or 'This is not permissible'. ... There is no middle course. The function of PO is to introduce a middle course ... PO is never a judgement. It does not quarrel with the verdict but with the very application of the judgement. PO is an anti-judgement device. PO allows one to hold an arrangement for a little longer without having to affirm or deny it. PO delays judgement."
>
> The three broad classes of PO use: "1. The generation of alternatives. Anti-arrogance. Relaxation. Reexamination of a concept. ... 2. Provocation. The use of arrangements of information as stimuli. Juxtapositions. Introduction of random words. ... 3. Protection and rescue. Holding off judgement. Temporarily reversing judgement. Removal of the NO label."
>
> — *Lateral Thinking: A Textbook of Creativity* (1970), ch. "The new word po" (lines 5790–5799, 6597–6606)

**Context**: PO 챕터. PO의 핵심 기능(판단 보류, 대안 양산, 도발/보호)을 세 부류로 정리.

### Q11 — Movement vs judgment, and "Provocation without movement is useless"
> "Everyone knows that instant judgment is the enemy of creativity." / "Judgment is the powerful tool we have for keeping on these routine tracks."
>
> "With judgment, we look at an idea and compare it to our experience. ... With movement, we use the idea for its movement value to go forward to a new idea." / "Movement is not just a suspension of judgment. Movement is an active mental process." / "Movement is a crucial part of lateral thinking. Provocation without movement is useless." / "The apparently crazy idea is not an end point, but only the first stage."
>
> "I invented the word 'PO' which stands for a provocation operation." / "The PO (provocation) serves to take us out of the comfort of an existing pattern."
>
> — *Serious Creativity* (1992), excerpted at debono.com/serious-creativity-article

**Context**: 드 보노가 movement를 "판단 보류"보다 강한 능동적 동작으로 재정의한 후기 정식화. 도발은 그 자체가 목적이 아니라 movement로 이어져야 한다는 핵심 규칙.

### Q12 — The five formal ways to set up a provocation
> "There are five formal ways of getting [provocations]: ... These include escape, reversal, exaggeration, distortion, and wishful thinking."
>
> Cheat-sheet definitions (verbatim from *Serious Creativity*):
> - **Escape/negation**: "Escape/negation of things that are taken for granted." (예: "Building software is free.")
> - **Reversal**: "Reversal (reversal of relationships; like escape, but narrower)." (예: "Servers call clients.")
> - **Exaggeration**: "Exaggeration (values become very large or small, etc)." (예: "Programs are a single line of code.")
> - **Distortion**: "Distortion of normal relationships (as in time or order)." (예: "The program is written before the requirements.")
> - **Wishful thinking**: "Wishful thinking (positive version of escape)." (예: "Wouldn't it be nice if programs wrote programs.")
>
> — *Serious Creativity* (1992); debono.com/serious-creativity-article + UCSD cheat-sheet PDF

**Context**: 도발(PO)을 "어떻게 만드는가"의 5가지 정식 절차. 즉흥적 발상이 아니라 당연한 것을 부정/거꾸로/과장/왜곡/희망사항으로 비트는 기계적 조작.

### Q13 — The five ways to get *movement* from a provocation
> Verbatim from cheat-sheet (from *Serious Creativity*):
> - "Expose the principle." (원칙/특징을 뽑아 거기서 전진)
> - "Expose the difference between provocation and starting point." (도발과 출발점의 차이에 주목)
> - "Moment-to-moment: Follow through with the consequences by simulation." (도발을 실행했을 때의 순간순간 결과를 따라가기)
> - "Look for the positives." (긍정적 측면을 찾기)
> - "Look for the circumstances in which the provocation is useful." (그 도발이 유용해지는 특수 상황을 찾기)
>
> — *Serious Creativity* (1992); UCSD cheat-sheet PDF + debono.com (movement methods list)

**Context**: 도발문에서 실제 아이디어를 뽑아내는 movement 5절차. PO 다음에 반드시 따라야 하는 후속 동작.

### Q14 — Random word: the exact procedure + a verbatim worked example
> "Random word stimulation. This is a practical and definite procedure ... If one is a purist one can use a table of random numbers to select a page in a dictionary. ... With less trouble one can simply think of two numbers and find the word that way. Or throw some dice. What one must not do is to open a dictionary and go through the pages until one finds a likely looking word. That would be selection and it would be useless from a random stimulation point of view."
>
> Worked example (problem = "the housing shortage", random word = "noose", three-minute timing):
> "noose – rope – suspension construction system – tentlike houses but made of permanent materials ...";
> "noose – loop – adjustable loop – what about adjustable round houses which could be expanded as required ...";
> "noose – snare – capture – capture a share of the labour market ..."
>
> "The word is used in order to get things going — not to prove anything." / "In the above example the time allowed was three minutes. This is quite long enough to stimulate ideas."
>
> — *Lateral Thinking: A Textbook of Creativity* (1970), ch. "Random stimulation" (lines 4900–4910, 4963–5025)

**Context**: random word 기법의 정확한 절차(진짜 무작위로 뽑되 고르지 말 것) + 실제 3분 발산 사례. "증명이 아니라 시동을 거는 것"이 목적.

### Q15 — Why random stimulation works (mechanism)
> "Random stimulation only works because the mind functions as a self-maximizing memory system. In such a system there is a limited and coherent attention span. This means that any two inputs cannot remain separate no matter how unconnected they are. ... But if both are deliberately held in attention ... then a connection will eventually form between the two."
>
> "Because of the way the mind works any stimulus whatsoever can be found to develop a connection with any other."
>
> — *Lateral Thinking: A Textbook of Creativity* (1970), ch. "Random stimulation" (lines 4918–4927, 5207–5208)

**Context**: 무작위 단어가 작동하는 이유 — 마음이 자기최대화 시스템이라 동시에 붙잡힌 두 입력은 결국 연결된다.

### Q16 — Reversal method: "kick hard against what is there"
> "In the reversal method one kicks hard against what is there and fixed in order to move away in the opposite direction." / "In the reversal method one takes things as they are and then turns them round, inside out, upside down, back to front. Then one sees what happens. It is a provocative rearrangement of information. You make water run uphill instead of downhill. Instead of driving a car the car leads you."
>
> "There is no one correct way [to reverse]. Nor should there be any search for some true reversal. Any sort of reversal will do." / "In lateral thinking one is not looking for the right answer but for a different arrangement of information which will provoke a different way of looking at the situation."
>
> — *Lateral Thinking: A Textbook of Creativity* (1970), ch. "The reversal method" (lines 3495–3538)

**Context**: reversal 기법. 막혔을 때 고정된 것을 거꾸로 차고 나가 출발 방향을 바꾸는 절차. '옳은 반전'을 찾는 게 아니라 어떤 반전이든 좋다.

### Q17 — Challenge: assumptions held by historical continuity, not validity
> "It is the purpose of lateral thinking to challenge any assumption ... General agreement about an assumption is no guarantee that it is correct. It is historical continuity that maintains most assumptions — not a repeated assessment of their validity."
>
> "In challenging assumptions one challenges the necessity of boundaries and limits and one challenges the validity of individual concepts. ... there is no question of attacking the assumptions as wrong. Nor is there any question of offering better alternatives. It is simply a matter of trying to restructure patterns."
>
> Official tool framing: "Challenge — Challenging traditional ways constructively / Learning to break free of our thinking patterns." / "Why is it done this way?"
>
> — *Lateral Thinking: A Textbook of Creativity* (1970), ch. "Challenging assumptions" (lines 2265–2343); De Bono Group tools page; UCSD cheat-sheet

**Context**: challenge 기법. "왜 꼭 이 방식이어야 하나"를 묻되, 틀렸다고 공격하거나 더 나은 대안을 들이미는 게 아니라 패턴 자체를 흔드는 동작.

### Q18 — Concept extraction / concept fan: breed ideas from the underlying concept
> Official tool framing: "Alternatives — Learning the 'how to' and value of extracting concepts / Using concepts to breed new ideas."
>
> Cheat-sheet (from *Serious Creativity*): "Concept Fan/Pull-back: Create alternatives by finding [the] concept behind [an] idea and generating [a] list of ideas from [the] concept, etc."
>
> — De Bono Group lateral-thinking tools page; UCSD cheat-sheet PDF (from *Serious Creativity*, 1992)

**Context**: concept extraction 기법. 구체 아이디어에서 그 밑의 개념을 뽑아낸 뒤, 그 개념으로 새 아이디어를 다시 양산하는 발산 절차.

### Q19 — Six Thinking Hats: each hat's function (verbatim)
> "The white hat is all about information. What information you have, what information you need and where to get it."
> "The red hat is about feelings, intuitions and instincts. The red hat invites feelings without justification."
> "The black hat identifies risk. It is used for critical judgment and must give the logical reasons for concerns."
> "The yellow hat is for a positive view of things. It looks for the benefits and values."
> "The green hat is for creative thinking and generating new ideas, alternatives, possibilities and new concepts."
> "The blue hat is the control hat. It is used for thinking about thinking. The blue hat sets the agenda, focus and sequence, ensures the guidelines are observed and asks for summaries, conclusions, decisions and plans action."
>
> — *Six Thinking Hats* (1985); debono.com/six-thinking-hats-summary

**Context**: 6모자 공식 정의. Green이 발산(creative/alternatives), Black이 비판, White가 사실, Red가 감정, Yellow가 이점, Blue가 사고 과정 관리.

### Q20 — One hat at a time; sequence opens and closes with Blue
> "The main idea is to have the group 'wear only one hat at a time'."
> "Blue hat is unique as it thinks about thinking ... Thinking sessions usually begin & end with blue hat ... Controls the sequence or use of other hats."
> Open with blue: "Why are we here? What are we thinking about? ... A plan for the sequence of hats." Close with blue: "What have we achieved? What decision have we reached? What do we do next?"
> "Sequence can be preset or evolving. Not necessary to use every hat."
> Design sequence example: "Open with the blue hat. Then, use the green hat to generate new ideas ... Follow up with some red hat (feelings) thinking. Close with the blue hat."
>
> — *Six Thinking Hats* (1985), summarized in K.S. Mathew deck (lines 182, 276–334, 367–375)

**Context**: 모자 사용 규칙 — 한 번에 한 모자, Blue로 열고 닫음, 시퀀스는 미리 짜거나 즉석으로. 발산(Design) 시퀀스는 Green을 가운데 둠.

### Q21 — Parallel thinking replaces argument
> "[Each] thinker puts forward his or her thoughts in parallel with the thoughts of others — not attacking the thoughts of others."
> "Traditional argument is totally useless for such a design process. Instead, we need Parallel Thinking." / "Adversarial confrontation is replaced by a cooperative exploration of the subject." / "Both A and B wear each hat together as they explore all sides of an issue."
> Mathew deck (traditional vs parallel): traditional = "argument / adversarial / western thinking ... 2400 years old ... Socrates, Plato and Aristotle ... concerned with 'what is'"; parallel = "concerned with 'what can be' ... designing a way forward ... Deals with only one aspect at a time."
>
> — De Bono Group parallel-thinking page; K.S. Mathew deck (lines 93–110)

**Context**: parallel thinking 정의 — 논쟁(공격/반박) 대신 모두가 같은 방향을 동시에 보는 협력적 탐색. Six Hats의 운영 철학.

## Critiques & limits (raw, 평가 금지)

### C1 — Lateral thinking is pseudoscience, derivative and untested
> 제목·요지(검색 스니펫 기준): "De Bono's idea of lateral thinking is nothing but classic pseudoscience, both derivative and untested." / "De Bono's popular theory is textbook pseudoscience: unsound, untested and derivative of real (unacknowledged) research." / "De Bono's reputation ... is founded on nifty puzzles, a storehouse of anecdotes, an abundance of imperial generalities, no end of clunking analogies and neologisms, and a toolkit that was by no means as novel as his publishers or readers might have assumed."
>
> — Antonio Melechi (요크대 사회학 명예연구원), *Aeon*, "Lateral thinking is classic pseudoscience, derivative and untested", 2020-06-11

**Target**: lateral thinking이 검증된 독창적 방법론이라는 주장 — 특히 "절차로 정식화했다"는 신뢰성과 독창성.
**Context**: 드 보노 사망(2021) 직전 발표된 장문 비판 에세이. 기법이 기존 창의성 연구의 재포장이며 통제 연구로 입증된 적이 없다는 논지.
**Translation (ko)**: "드 보노의 수평적 사고는 전형적 사이비 과학으로, 파생적이고 검증되지 않았다." / "그의 명성은 깔끔한 퍼즐, 일화 더미, 거창한 일반화, 끝없는 어설픈 비유와 신조어, 그리고 출판사나 독자가 믿었던 만큼 새롭지 않은 도구 모음에 기대고 있다."
**(주의: Aeon 본문 전체는 서버 429로 직접 회수 실패 — Gaps 참조. 위 문장들은 검색 스니펫에서 인용.)**

### C2 — Lateral thinking lacks evidence; creativity is "ordinary thought processes"
> "Robert Weisberg ... argues that there is insufficient evidence for the effectiveness of lateral thinking and that the creative process is better described as a process of logical thinking, trial and error, feedback, and reflection."
> "Weisberg argues that there is very little evidence of lateral thinking in the work of great creators such as Mozart, Beethoven, Darwin, Edison ... For him, the idea of extraordinary creative thinking processes is part of 'the Genius myth'."
> "He argues that the creative process takes place via ... 'ordinary thought processes' rather than lateral thinking."
>
> — Robert Weisberg (cognitive psychologist), 요약: Lateral Action, "Lateral Thinking: Is it Necessary for Creativity?"

**Target**: "lateral thinking이 창의적인 사람과 그렇지 않은 사람을 가른다"는 드 보노의 핵심 주장.
**Context**: 인지심리학자 Weisberg가 위대한 창작자들의 작업에서 별도의 '측면적' 사고 과정 증거를 거의 못 찾았다고 주장. 창의는 평범한 논리·시행착오·피드백의 산물이라는 입장.
**Translation (ko)**: "위대한 창작자들의 작업에서 lateral thinking의 증거는 거의 없다. ... 창의 과정은 lateral thinking이 아니라 '평범한 사고 과정'을 통해 일어난다."

### C3 — Concerned with usefulness, not with testing validity
> "Those taking this approach have been concerned primarily with developing creativity, secondarily with understanding it, but almost not at all with testing the validity of their ideas about it."
>
> "[De Bono] is more interested in the usefulness of developing ideas than proving the reliability or efficacy of his approach."
>
> — Robert J. Sternberg (1998); Moseley et al., *Frameworks for Thinking* (2006) — Wikipedia 재인용

**Target**: 드 보노 방법론의 실증적 타당성 — 효과가 측정·검증됐는가.
**Context**: 학술 평가. 드 보노 진영이 창의 '개발'에 집중하느라 자기 주장의 타당성 검증은 거의 하지 않았다는 지적.
**Translation (ko)**: "이 접근을 취한 이들은 주로 창의성을 개발하는 데 관심을 뒀고, 그것을 이해하는 데는 부차적으로, 자기 생각의 타당성을 검증하는 데는 거의 관심이 없었다." / "그는 자기 접근의 신뢰성·효능을 증명하기보다 아이디어 개발의 유용성에 더 관심이 있다."

### C4 — Sparse evidence that CoRT / Thinking Hats generalize
> "There is sparse research evidence to show that generalised improvements in thinking performance can be attributed to training in the use of CoRT or Thinking Hats tools."
>
> (Wikipedia 재인용: CoRT 호주 원주민 아동 대상 연구에서 "little evidence of generalisation")
>
> — Moseley et al., *Frameworks for Thinking* (2006) — Wikipedia 재인용

**Target**: 드 보노 도구(CoRT·Six Hats) 훈련이 일반적 사고력 향상으로 이어진다는 주장.
**Context**: 사고력 교육 프로그램들을 검토한 학술서. 효과가 특정 과제를 넘어 일반화된다는 통제 연구 증거가 빈약하다는 평가.
**Translation (ko)**: "CoRT나 Thinking Hats 도구 훈련이 일반적인 사고 수행 향상으로 이어진다는 연구 증거는 빈약하다."

### C5 — Total elimination of conflict is "out of tune with modern thinking"
> "De Bono's approach to total elimination of conflict is completely out of tune with modern thinking and, therefore, unsatisfactory."
>
> — M. Afzalur Rahim (2011), summarizing/critiquing *Six Thinking Hats* (1985) — Wikipedia 재인용

**Target**: parallel thinking이 논쟁(adversarial)을 대체해야 한다는 주장 — 갈등을 제거 대상으로 보는 프레임.
**Context**: 갈등관리 연구자가 Six Hats의 "갈등 제거" 지향이 현대 갈등관리론(갈등의 생산적 측면 인정)과 맞지 않는다고 평가.
**Translation (ko)**: "갈등을 전면 제거하려는 드 보노의 접근은 현대적 사고와 완전히 어긋나 있어 불만족스럽다."

### C6 — Six Hats: facilitator-dependent, can be gamed, groupthink risk
> "[The method] is only as effective as its facilitator, and an overly ego-driven leader could use their blue hat role to shut down criticism or unwanted ideas."
> "The method may also struggle with groupthink, as diversity of backgrounds and viewpoints is needed to prevent participants from misinterpreting which assumptions are fact-based versus matters of faith or probability."
>
> — 비판 요약 (검색 결과: hptbydts blog / The Decision Lab 등 2차 정리)

**Target**: Six Hats가 더 나은 사고를 보장한다는 운영상의 주장.
**Context**: 실무 적용에서 드러나는 한계 — Blue hat을 쥔 진행자가 비판을 봉쇄하거나 집단사고를 유발할 수 있음.
**Translation (ko)**: "이 방법은 진행자 역량만큼만 효과적이며, 에고가 강한 리더는 blue hat 역할로 비판을 차단할 수 있다." / "배경·관점의 다양성이 없으면 집단사고에 빠질 수 있다."

## Recurring observations (raw, 해석 금지)
- "generative vs selective"가 lateral/vertical 대비의 1번 항목으로 반복(Q1, Q4). vertical = select/exclude/judge, lateral = generate/open/move.
- "movement"라는 단어가 1970년판부터 1992년판까지 반복되며, 후기로 갈수록 "판단 보류 < 능동적 동작"으로 강화(Q2, Q11).
- "PO is never a judgement / anti-judgement device"가 PO 챕터에서 반복(Q9, Q10). 판단(NO)의 정확한 거울상으로 PO를 배치.
- "self-organizing / self-maximizing memory system" 표현이 메커니즘 정당화마다 반복 등장(Q6, Q7, Q15).
- "There is no one correct way / any X will do" 패턴이 reversal·random word에서 반복(Q14, Q16) — 옳음이 아니라 자극이 목적.
- 기법마다 "당연한 것(taken for granted)을 부정/거꾸로/과장/왜곡한다"는 동일 골격(Q12, Q16, Q17).
- 도발(provocation) → movement → harvesting의 2단계 구조가 PO·도발 5기법·movement 5기법에서 반복(Q11, Q12, Q13).
- 6모자에서 발산은 Green 하나에 격리되고, 판단/비판은 Black으로 분리(Q19) — "한 번에 한 모드".
- 비판 진영의 반복 키워드: "untested / no (rigorous) evidence / derivative / usefulness over validity / generalisation"(C1–C4).
